UUID Error
Acceptance

FH Development uses Product UUIDs to name products, its a Universal Unique Identification system for everything we do. When you get this error, it means either the script has been tampered with or that our developers & exectuvies have changed bits around. Developers may have put in the wrong UUID or executives hay have changed the product UUID in the Master Settings where FH Development runs modules, traces, callbacks and endpoints through.

To solve this, you can:

  • Re-add the product file,
  • Contact support via our discord server
